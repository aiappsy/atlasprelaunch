### 1. The Core Legal Commitment:
When Founder Pål Juritzen (pronounced "Paul Yur-it-zen") established ATLAS Travel Club under HARD ROCK CAPITAL LTD, he instituted an ironclad contractual rule: a member must never lose money by joining the club. Every membership is backed by an enforceable 100% Value Guarantee.

### 2. The Two-Track Qualification Structure:
To be commercially airtight and protect against opportunistic exploitation, the guarantee operates across two clear tracks:

#### Track A: The Single-Booking Guarantee (High-Arbitrage Lodging)
A member is entitled to an immediate 100% refund of their annual dues on their very first completed booking, provided that booking is in a high-arbitrage lodging category where 20% to 45% retail margins exist:
1. Wholesale Hotel Stays: A completed reservation of a minimum of 2 consecutive nights at a participating wholesale bedbank hotel with a total booking value of at least €150 (ca. 1 700 NOK).
2. Vacation Resort Condominiums: Any completed 7-night resort condominium stay via RVC liquidation (studios, 1-bed, or 2-bed units sleeping 2 to 6 guests).
3. The Condition: If verified wholesale net savings on that single trip do not exceed the €110 (1 290 NOK) membership fee compared to public retail rates on Booking.com or Expedia for the identical dates and room category, ATLAS refunds 100% of the membership fee immediately.

#### Track B: The Cumulative Annual Guarantee (Flights & All Other Travel)
For all other travel categories—including flights, short 1-night hotel stays, and car rentals:
1. All bookings made during the 12-month membership period count toward the member's Cumulative Annual Net Savings.
2. If at the end of the 12-month membership year, total combined savings across all bookings do not exceed €110 (1 290 NOK), ATLAS refunds 100% of the membership fee upon request.

### 3. Explicit Anti-Exploitation Guardrails (The Flight Reality):
- Airlines operate on razor-thin net margins of 2% to 6%. ATLAS passes unbundled net carrier fares with 0% markup, saving members 8% to 14% (typically €15 to €40 per ticket in avoided GDS surcharges and baggage inflation).
- A member cannot claim an immediate €110 refund based on a single cheap €45 flight ticket. Flight savings accumulate under Track B toward the annual total.
- High-margin lodging (20% to 45% margins) easily covers the €110 fee on trip one: a 3-night Paris hotel saves €270+, and a 7-night resort condo from €299 saves €1,350+. Over 98% of members recoup their fee on their first qualifying lodging trip.

### 4. Zero Friction Claim Protocol:
- Member submits a claim within 30 days of completing their qualifying Track A stay (or at the end of their 12-month membership for Track B).
- Requires a single screenshot or link showing the higher retail rate on Booking.com or Expedia for the identical dates and room type at the time of booking.
- Hard Rock Capital Ltd verifies and issues a 100% refund directly to the member's payment card within 2 business days.
- The member retains their earned Rewards Vault points and their complimentary €480 Connex 3-Day European Hotel Break voucher.
