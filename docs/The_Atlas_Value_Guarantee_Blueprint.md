## 2. Qualifying Thresholds: What Triggers the Guarantee

The 100% Value Guarantee operates under two distinct, mathematically sound tracks:

### Track A: The Single-Booking Guarantee (High-Arbitrage Lodging)
A member is entitled to an immediate 100% refund of their annual dues on their **very first completed booking**, provided that booking is in a high-arbitrage lodging category where 20% to 45% retail margins exist:
1. **Wholesale Hotel Stays:** A completed reservation of a minimum of **2 consecutive nights** at a participating wholesale bedbank hotel (RateHawk, WebBeds, Hotelbeds) with a total baseline booking value of at least **€150 (ca. 1 700 NOK)**.
2. **Vacation Resort Condominiums (RVC):** Any completed **7-night resort condominium stay** (studio suites, 1-bedroom, or 2-bedroom units).
3. **Requirement:** If the verified wholesale rate paid through ATLAS on that single qualifying trip did not deliver net cash savings exceeding **€110 (1 290 NOK)** compared to the publicly available retail rate on Booking.com or Expedia, the member is refunded 100% of their annual membership fee.

### Track B: The Cumulative Annual Travel Guarantee (All Travel Categories)
For all other travel—including flights, short 1-night hotel stays, and car rentals:
1. **Universal Inclusions:** All bookings made during the 12-month membership period (including unbundled NDC flights, domestic rail, rental cars, and hotels) count toward the member's **Cumulative Annual Net Savings**.
2. **Annual Audit Standard:** If at the end of the member's 12-month membership year, the total combined net savings realized across all completed bookings does not exceed **€110 (1 290 NOK)**, ATLAS refunds 100% of the annual membership fee upon request.

## 3. Explicit Carve-Outs & Fair-Use Guardrails (Anti-Exploitation)

To protect the financial integrity of HARD ROCK CAPITAL LTD, the following explicit restrictions apply:

### 1. The Economy Flight Carve-Out (Thin Margins)
* **Reality:** Airlines operate on razor-thin net carrier margins (2% to 6%). ATLAS passes unbundled net fares with **0% markup**, saving members 8% to 14% (typically **€15 to €40 per ticket** in avoided GDS booking surcharges and baggage inflation).
* **The Rule:** A member **CANNOT** claim an instant €110 single-booking refund based on a single cheap economy flight ticket (e.g., a €45 flight where the net saving was €15). 
* **Fair-Use Treatment:** Net savings from flights accumulate toward the **Track B Cumulative Annual Savings** total.

### 2. Standalone Single 1-Night Budget Bookings
* Single-night stays under €100 (hostels, budget motels, airport pods) do not qualify for Track A single-booking refunds, but accumulate under Track B.

### 3. Canceled, Refunded, or No-Show Bookings
* The guarantee applies strictly to **completed travel**. Stays that are canceled, refunded by the supplier, or resulted in a guest no-show are ineligible for value claims.

## 4. The "Apples-to-Apples" Retail Price Verification Standard

To ensure objective and fair resolution, price comparisons are evaluated against the following strict benchmark:

| Parameter | ATLAS Benchmark Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Comparison Platforms** | Public retail rates displayed on **Booking.com**, **Expedia**, or the hotel’s own public website. |
| **Timing of Comparison** | Rates must be verifiable at the time of booking (or within 24 hours of booking). |
| **Identical Property & Room** | Same hotel/resort, same room category, same bedding layout, and same view tier. |
| **Identical Dates** | Exact same check-in date and check-out date. |
| **Identical Terms** | Same cancellation policy (e.g., non-refundable vs. free cancellation) and same meal inclusions (e.g., room-only vs. breakfast included). |
| **Currency & Taxes** | All rates compared in the same currency with all mandatory local taxes and resort fees included. |
