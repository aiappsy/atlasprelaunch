**RACHEL:**  
This is the second core pillar of the tech stack: **B2B Wholesale Bedbanks**. ATLAS connects directly via institutional APIs to global bedbanks like **Hotelbeds**, **WebBeds**, and **RateHawk**. 

For listeners who haven't worked in the travel industry: a bedbank is an institutional wholesale clearinghouse. They negotiate bulk, contracted room allocations with more than 1.2 million properties across 190 countries. They supply corporate travel departments, luxury travel agencies, and behind the scenes, they even supply the major public booking sites.

**MICHAEL:**  
And why can’t an ordinary consumer just go to a bedbank website and book a room?

**RACHEL:**  
Because of a legal concept called **Rate Parity**. Major public booking platforms require hotels to sign clauses promising they will not publicly advertise a lower rate on an open website. If a boutique hotel in Barcelona or Paris lists a room for €200 on Booking.com, they are legally barred from displaying that room for €140 on an open Google search.

**MICHAEL:**  
But there is a legal exemption to Rate Parity, right?

**RACHEL:**  
A massive legal exemption recognized across Europe and internationally: **Closed-User Groups**. When inventory is placed behind an authenticated, password-protected membership wall that charges annual dues—the exact model Costco uses for retail goods—Rate Parity does not apply. Hoteliers and bedbanks are 100% legally permitted to distribute unbundled B2B net rates to verified members.
